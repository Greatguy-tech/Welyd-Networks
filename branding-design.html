03 · Show what is inside

Packaging that stands out on shelf and in hand.

Help customers understand and choose your product with packaging that balances brand recognition, clear information, and the realities of production.

Packaging applications

  • Labels, cartons, sleeves, and product wraps
  • Packaging families for different sizes or flavours
  • Product inserts, thank-you cards, and care notes
  • Retail-ready and gift packaging concepts

Design considerations

  • Information hierarchy and legibility
  • Brand consistency across a product range
  • Printer dielines and production constraints
  • Digital mockups for review and promotion
Useful for: food, beauty, retail, and makers preparing a first product, a new range, or a more polished unboxing experience.

Space and event materials

  • Exhibition stands, backdrops, and banners

Project support

05 · Show up consistently

Social media graphics made for each channel.

Make your posts recognisable and easy to understand in a fast-moving feed, without forcing every message into the same template.

Content formats

  • Campaign announcements and promotions
  • Product highlights, launches, and offers
  • Carousel explainers and educational graphics
  • Stories, reels covers, and profile headers

Ways to keep it useful

  • Reusable post templates for your team
  • Platform-sized exports and safe-area checks
  • Clear text hierarchy and on-brand imagery
  • Creative variations for a campaign or series
Useful for: businesses that publish regularly, run promotions, or want social posts that look connected instead of improvised.
