Make the most of the room

A little preparation goes a long way.

Good event participation is intentional. Decide what would make the time worthwhile before you register or book a stand.

Set a goal

Choose a clear outcome: meet buyers, learn a skill, find a partner, or introduce a product.

Prepare your story

Explain who you help and what you offer in a short, natural introduction.

Be present

Ask thoughtful questions, listen closely, and exchange details with permission.

Follow through

Send a personal follow-up soon after and agree on a useful next step.
