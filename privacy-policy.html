Your information

Privacy policy.

Here is what this website currently does with information and what to expect when you contact us.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy describes the Welyd Networks website’s current information practices. We aim to handle personal information in accordance with applicable data-protection law, including Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019. The business identity, contact details, service providers, and retention arrangements should be confirmed and this policy reviewed before the website is used to collect live enquiries.

Information you provide

The website includes an enquiry form that asks for details such as your name, company, email, phone number, services of interest, project description, and optional budget, timeline, source, and reference link. The form currently is not connected to a submission provider; it validates entries in your browser and displays a notice instead of sending them. Do not enter sensitive personal information in the form.

If you contact us by email, phone, or a third-party messaging service, we receive the information you choose to share. Newsletter sign-up is currently a demonstration only: an entered email address is validated in your browser, is not submitted to a mailing list, and is not saved by this website.

Information handled by your browser

If you switch the site’s light or dark theme, the site stores that preference in your browser’s local storage under “welyd-theme.” The Welyd Merchandise preview also saves your selected products, colours, sizes, and quantities in your browser’s local storage under “welyd-merch-cart” so the bag remains available on that browser. The bag is not submitted to Welyd Networks, and the store does not process orders or payments. The separate checkout page uses your contact and delivery details to prepare an email draft in your browser; the website does not store or submit those details. The custom merchandise request builder works the same way. If you choose to send either draft, your email provider handles it and delivers it to the address shown. You can clear saved browser values through your browser’s site-data settings. The website does not currently use its own analytics or advertising cookies.

Information collected by service providers

The site loads Google Fonts from Google and some images from Magnific. Your browser connects directly to those services to retrieve the resources, so those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details. The site also links to external social networks and WhatsApp; visiting those services is subject to their own privacy policies.

The website’s hosting provider may process technical request and security logs when you visit. The specific host and its retention practices should be confirmed by the website operator.

How information may be used

Information you send directly to us may be used to respond to you, discuss or provide requested services, keep appropriate business records, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information. If the enquiry form or newsletter is connected to a provider in the future, that provider and the information transferred should be identified here before the feature is enabled.

Retention and security

We keep information received directly only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose it was provided, including follow-up, legitimate business records, and legal requirements. The precise retention periods and deletion process should be confirmed by the operator. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access or correct personal information we hold about you, request deletion, object to or seek limits on certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya. Contact us using the details below to make a request; identity verification may be needed.

Children

This website is intended for businesses and general audiences, not for children. Please do not submit a child’s personal information through the site.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy if website features or data practices change. The latest version will appear on this page with an updated date. For privacy questions or requests, email hello@welydnetworks.co.ke. This contact address is shown as a placeholder on the website and must be confirmed before launch.
