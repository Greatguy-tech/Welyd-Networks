01 · Start with the people using it

Discovery and planning before the build.

Set a useful scope by understanding your customers, business priorities, existing content, and the tasks visitors need to complete.

Plan a website project →

Planning work

  • Goals, audience, and user-task workshop
  • Review of existing site and content
  • Page inventory and sitemap
  • Feature and integration requirements

Project clarity

  • Page and functionality scope
  • Content and asset requirements
  • Technical assumptions and dependencies
  • Milestones, review points, and launch plan
Useful for: new websites, redesigns, migrations, and teams that need to agree what the site must do before selecting a platform or starting development.

02 · Explain your offer clearly

Business and organization websites built to earn trust.

Give customers a reliable place to understand your services, learn about your team, see useful proof, and make an enquiry.

Common pages

  • Home, about, and service pages
  • Portfolio, case studies, or project galleries
  • Team, careers, and organization information
  • Contact, location, and frequently asked questions

Core experience

  • Responsive layouts for mobile and desktop
  • Clear navigation and calls to action
  • Accessible headings, contrast, and controls
  • Search-friendly page structure and metadata
Useful for: professional services, retailers, schools, NGOs, startups, and established businesses replacing a dated or unclear online presence.

03 · Make it easy to buy

E-commerce experiences that reduce friction.

Help customers browse, compare, and purchase products through a store experience that makes important information easy to find.

Storefront needs

  • Product categories and collection pages
  • Product details, variants, and imagery
  • Cart, checkout, and payment configuration
  • Delivery, returns, and customer information

Operational planning

  • Inventory and fulfilment considerations
  • Order and customer notification flows
  • Store administration and content handover
  • Analytics and conversion measurement setup
Scope note: platform fees, payment provider charges, product data preparation, and third-party integrations are agreed separately to suit your operation.

04 · Give a campaign one destination

Campaign and lead-generation landing pages.

Focus a visitor on a single offer or action, with a message and page structure aligned to the campaign that brought them there.

Landing page applications

  • Lead-generation and consultation offers
  • Event, registration, and webinar pages
  • Product launches and limited-time campaigns
  • Download, waitlist, and sign-up pages

Conversion foundations

  • Clear value proposition and benefit hierarchy
  • Relevant proof points and frequently asked questions
  • Focused forms and clear next-step messaging
  • Analytics and campaign tracking considerations
Useful for: campaigns that need a focused destination rather than sending every visitor to a general homepage.

05 · Make every page easier to use

User experience and content working together.

Organise information around visitor needs, and make each page clear, readable, and straightforward to navigate on any screen.

Experience design

  • Information architecture and navigation
  • Wireframes and responsive page layouts
  • Forms, menus, and interaction patterns
  • Accessibility and usability checks

Content support

  • Page purpose, headings, and content hierarchy
  • Calls to action and contact pathways
  • Content migration and editing guidance
  • Image selection, alt text, and media direction
Good UX: helps visitors complete important tasks and helps your team keep useful information accurate after launch.

06 · Launch with confidence

Launch, handover, and ongoing improvement.

A successful website project includes the checks, access, and practical guidance your team needs to manage the site and plan its next improvements.

Launch checks

  • Responsive and cross-browser review
  • Form, links, and key journey checks
  • Metadata, sitemap, and analytics review
  • Performance and accessibility basics

After launch

  • CMS and content editing walkthrough
  • Credentials and asset handover
  • Maintenance and hosting support options
  • Prioritised roadmap for future updates
Important: ongoing hosting, maintenance, paid software, and third-party services are scoped clearly so responsibilities are understood.
